Careers

Work with Hayakkom

We work with freelancers and staff across Egypt who look after Gulf patients and their families on the ground. If you are good with people and reliable under pressure, we want to hear from you.

How we work

Before you apply

01

Most roles are freelance or per-assignment

We call you when a patient journey needs your role, agree the scope and the fee for that assignment, and pay on completion. Some roles become regular once trust is established.

02

Arabic and English matter

Almost every client is a Gulf Arabic speaker. Fluent Arabic is essential for most roles, and functional English helps considerably.

03

Discretion is non-negotiable

You will encounter patients' medical information and private circumstances. Anyone who discusses a client outside the assignment will not work with us again. For most roles you will be asked to sign a confidentiality undertaking.

04

Reliability over experience

We would rather work with someone punctual, honest and calm than someone with an impressive CV who cancels the night before.

Open roles

Roles we recruit for

🗺️

Tour guides

Freelance, per assignment

Guiding Gulf families and patient companions around Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, Alexandria and the coastal cities. Often shorter, gentler itineraries than standard tourism because the group is travelling around a medical appointment.

What we need
•Valid Egyptian tour guide licence
•Fluent Arabic; English an advantage
•Strong knowledge of at least one region
•Patience with families travelling with someone unwell
•Flexibility — medical schedules shift
Apply for this role
🤝

Patient companions & caretakers

Freelance or regular

Accompanying patients to appointments, waiting with them, helping with hospital paperwork, checking in during recovery, and being the reassuring presence for someone in an unfamiliar country. One of the roles clients value most.

What we need
•Nursing, care or hospitality background preferred
•Fluent Arabic
•Genuine patience and a calm manner
•Comfortable in hospital environments
•Willing to sign a confidentiality undertaking
•Female applicants especially encouraged — many clients request a female companion
Apply for this role
🚘

Drivers

Freelance, per assignment

Airport pick-ups, hospital and clinic transfers, inter-city journeys and day trips. Post-operative patients need smooth, unhurried driving and help getting in and out of the vehicle.

What we need
•Valid Egyptian licence and clean record
•Insured, air-conditioned, genuinely clean vehicle
•Punctuality — this is the single most common complaint in the industry
•Basic Arabic and courteous manner; English an advantage
•Discretion about who you are carrying and why
•Knowledge of Cairo and Giza hospital locations
Apply for this role
📈

Marketers & performance specialists

Freelance, retainer or project

Arabic-first campaigns aimed at Saudi, UAE and Kuwaiti audiences. Paid search and social, landing pages, funnel and conversion work, CRM and enquiry-handling flows, SEO and content in Arabic.

What we need
•Demonstrable results with GCC audiences — bring numbers, not slides
•Native or near-native Arabic copywriting
•Hands-on with Meta and Google Ads
•Healthcare or medical tourism experience an advantage
•Understanding that medical advertising must stay honest and compliant
Apply for this role
🎥

Content creators & influencers

Per campaign or collaboration

Short-form video and photography in Egypt for Gulf audiences — travel, wellness and lifestyle content. We also work with creators who can document a journey tastefully and without overstating outcomes.

What we need
•Genuine Gulf audience — we will ask for real analytics, not follower counts
•Arabic-language content
•Ability to produce to brief and to deadline
•Willingness to disclose paid partnerships as required
•Absolutely no promising of medical results in content
Apply for this role
🗂️

Patient coordinators

Part-time or full-time

The core of the business. Handling enquiries from first WhatsApp message through to the patient returning home — collecting records, liaising with clinics, building itineraries, and being the single point of contact the client trusts.

What we need
•Excellent written and spoken Arabic; good English
•Highly organised, able to hold many moving parts
•Calm and clear under pressure
•Medical, hospitality or customer service background
•Comfortable with WhatsApp, email and spreadsheets
•Based in Egypt, or Gulf-based with Egypt knowledge
Apply for this role
Applying

How to apply

Email us with the role in the subject line. Tell us plainly what you have done, where you are based, your languages, and your rate or salary expectation. Attach a CV, licence or portfolio where you have one. We reply to applications we can use; if you do not hear back within two weeks, we did not have a fit at that time.
