North Coast (Sahel) Egypt: Summer Guide for Gulf Families

Turquoise Mediterranean water, gated resorts and family chalets — where to stay, when to go, what it costs and how to book it safely.

In short: Egypt's North Coast — known as the Sahel — is the Mediterranean shore west of Alexandria, from Sidi Abdel Rahman and El Alamein to Ras El Hekma and Marsa Matrouh. It is Egypt's summer coast: the season runs roughly June to September, and most stays are in gated resorts, hotels and private chalets. Resort rooms cost about 690–1,880 SAR a night in summer, and private chalets or villas about 900–3,750 SAR a night.

Where to stay

New El AlameinMarassiSidi Abdel RahmanRas El HekmaAlmaza BayMarsa Matrouh & Agiba Beach

New El Alamein — the newest city on the coast, with large hotels, a long promenade and the most activity in July and August. Marassi — a large gated resort with a marina, beaches and chalets or villas for families who want everything inside one gate. Sidi Abdel Rahman and Ras El Hekma — quieter bays with some of the clearest water. Almaza Bay and Marsa Matrouh — further west, calmer, with Agiba Beach among the most photographed spots in Egypt.

When to go

The season is June to September. July and August are the peak — book early. June and September are calmer, better value and still warm enough for the beach. Outside summer many resorts are quiet.

Getting there

Most families drive: around three to four hours from Cairo to the El Alamein area. A private car with driver is the easiest option with children and luggage. El Alamein International Airport receives seasonal flights — check current schedules from your city before planning around it.

Tips for a smooth summer

  1. Book July and August early — the best chalets go months ahead.
  2. Ask for the rental contract, entry permits and deposit terms in writing before you pay anything.
  3. Respect beach flags: the Mediterranean can have waves and currents on windy days.
  4. Choose a resort with a pool and shade if you travel with young children.

Questions families ask

Is the North Coast good for families?

Yes — most stays are in gated resorts with beaches, pools and restaurants inside, which is why many Egyptian and Gulf families choose it for the summer.

When is the North Coast season?

Roughly June to September. July and August are the busiest and most expensive months; June and September are calmer and better value.

How far is the North Coast from Cairo?

Around three to four hours by road to the El Alamein area, depending on traffic and the exact resort.

Do I need a permit to enter the gated resorts?

Some gated resorts require entry permits and a deposit for rented chalets and villas. We check the rental contract, permits and deposit terms in writing before you pay.
