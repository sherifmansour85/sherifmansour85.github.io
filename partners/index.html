Partnerships

Partner with Hayakkom

We coordinate Gulf patients and corporate clients into Egypt. If you provide the care, the facility or the ground services, there is a clear way to work with us.

Why partner

What a Hayakkom partnership actually gives you

01

Qualified demand, not cold leads

Enquiries reach you after a coordinator has already established what the patient needs, where they are travelling from, their dates and their budget range. You receive a prepared case, not a phone number.

02

Arabic-first handling

We handle the Gulf-side conversation end to end in Arabic — enquiry, documents, expectations, logistics — so your team receives the patient ready and briefed.

03

Journey logistics handled

Airport transfers, accommodation, appointment sequencing, companions and follow-up coordination are managed by us. Your team focuses on the clinical or service delivery.

04

Honest representation

We publish only what can be verified. We will not invent accreditations, ratings or outcomes on your behalf, and we will ask you for documentation before publishing any credential.

05

One accountable contact

You deal with a named coordinator rather than a switchboard, with a single point of escalation if something changes.

Two partnership tracks

Which one describes you?

🏥
Track 1

Healthcare providers

Hospitals · Specialist clinics · Dental centres · Hair restoration clinics · Bariatric and cosmetic surgery centres · Fertility centres · Diagnostic and imaging centres · Wellness and rehabilitation facilities

What we look for
✓Valid Egyptian Ministry of Health licensing for the facility
✓Licensed, credentialled treating physicians with verifiable registration
✓Willingness to provide written, itemised estimates before the patient travels
✓Capacity to review medical records remotely before travel
✓Any international accreditation, where it genuinely exists
✓Arabic-speaking clinical or reception staff, or openness to our coordinator attending
✓A named contact for international patients
What we provide
→Pre-qualified Gulf patients with records already collected
→Coordinated appointment scheduling around the patient's travel window
→Patient transport to and from your facility
→Arabic interpretation during appointments where needed
→Post-treatment follow-up coordination after the patient returns home
→Your facility profile on our site — published only with your approval

Track 2

Tourism, hospitality & ground services

Licensed tour operators · Hotels and serviced apartments · Transport and chauffeur companies · Nile cruise operators · Red Sea and coastal resorts · DMCs · Conference venues and AV suppliers · Catering companies

What we look for
✓Valid Egyptian tourism or commercial licensing for your activity
✓Recovery-appropriate facilities where relevant — lift access, quiet rooms, room service
✓Reliable, documented pricing we can quote from
✓Vehicles and drivers that are insured, clean and punctual
✓Arabic-speaking staff or guides
✓Experience with Gulf families, or genuine willingness to learn what they expect
✓Ability to accommodate schedule changes — medical timelines move
What we provide
→Steady referral volume tied to medical journeys rather than seasonal tourism
→Advance booking with clear written requirements
→Longer average stays — medical patients typically stay 7–14 nights
→Family and companion bookings alongside the patient
→Direct coordination with one contact instead of scattered requests
Apply as a tourism or service partner
The process

How partnership applications are handled

01

You send your details

Email us with your company name, licence details, specialities or services, location, and a contact person. Attach your licence and any accreditation documents.

02

We review and verify

We check licensing and, for healthcare providers, physician credentials. We may ask for additional documentation. We do not skip this step.

03

Commercial discussion

A call to agree scope, rates, capacity, response times and how enquiries will be passed between us. Put in writing on both sides.

04

Trial period

We start with a small number of cases to test communication and service quality before scaling volume.

05

Listing and referral

Once established, your profile may be published on our site with your written approval, and you enter the regular referral rotation.
