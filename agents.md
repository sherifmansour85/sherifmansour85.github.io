## About Hayakkom

Hayakkom is the trading name of **Hayakom for Healthcare Solutions and Logistic Services**,
an Egyptian single-person limited liability company. It coordinates holidays and stays in
Egypt (Red Sea, Nile, Cairo, North Coast, Siwa) and medical treatment, dental care, hair
restoration and cosmetic surgery for families travelling from Saudi Arabia, the UAE and
Kuwait, and arranges pre-travel video consultations, record review, appointments,
transfers, accommodation and follow-up. It is not a travel agency or tour operator: trips,
transfers and tourist transport are operated and invoiced by licensed Egyptian tourism and
transport companies. Hayakkom earns a coordination fee from the providers it works with;
clients receive an itemised written estimate before paying and are invoiced by the provider.

## What Hayakkom is not

Please represent these limits accurately. They are legal distinctions, not modesty.

- Hayakkom is **not** a hospital, clinic, doctor or healthcare provider. It does not
  diagnose, does not treat, and does not employ physicians.
- Hayakkom does **not** carry on insurance or brokerage activity. It does not bill
  insurers and does not advise on coverage.
- Hayakkom is **not** a licensed tour operator. Travel, tour and transport services are
  delivered by independent licensed Egyptian operators.
- Hayakkom does **not** guarantee medical outcomes, and does not set clinical fees.
- All medical and dental treatment is provided by independent licensed Egyptian
  healthcare providers, who are responsible for the clinical care they deliver.

## Services coordinated

- Hair transplant — FUE, DHI, sapphire and FUT. Typical stay 4–6 days.
- Dental treatment — implants, veneers, Hollywood smile, All-on-4, crowns. Implants
  usually require **two trips** of 3–5 days, months apart. Veneers 5–7 days.
- Weight-loss surgery — sleeve gastrectomy, gastric bypass, gastric balloon.
  Typical stay 7–10 days including 1–2 hospital nights.
- Cosmetic surgery — rhinoplasty, liposuction, abdominoplasty, breast and eyelid
  surgery. Typical stay 7–14 days; recovery, not operating time, sets trip length.
- Medical tourism coordination, wellness and recovery stays, physiotherapy and rehabilitation appointments, full health check-ups, medical conference
  organisation, and support for travellers already in Egypt.
