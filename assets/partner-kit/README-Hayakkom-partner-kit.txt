HAYAKKOM PARTNER KIT — what each file is for
=============================================

Thank you for partnering with Hayakkom. These files are for your website team (webmaster) so you can show
the partnership on your website and social media. Please use them as they are, without changing colours or wording.

1. hayakkom-logo.png
   The official Hayakkom logo. Use it on a "Partners" or "International patients" page, or in a post announcing
   the partnership. Keep some empty space around it and do not stretch or recolour it.

2. hayakkom-partner-badge.png / hayakkom-partner-badge.svg
   A "Partner of Hayakkom" badge. Put it in your website footer or on your international-patients page.
   The PNG works everywhere; the SVG stays sharp at any size if your website supports it.

3. hayakkom-partner-snippet.html
   Ready-to-paste website code: it shows the badge and links it to www.hayakkom.com.
   Your webmaster pastes it into the page where the badge should appear. No other setup is needed.

4. Short description you may use (English)
   "We are a partner of Hayakkom, which coordinates treatment and travel to Egypt for families from Saudi Arabia
   and the Gulf. Patients referred by Hayakkom are treated by our licensed medical team."

   الوصف المختصر (عربي)
   "نحن شريك لـ حياكم، التي تنسّق رحلات العلاج والسفر إلى مصر للعائلات من السعودية والخليج.
   يتلقى المرضى المحالون عن طريق حياكم العلاج على يد فريقنا الطبي المرخّص."

Please note: Hayakkom coordinates trips; it is not a hospital, clinic or travel agency. Please describe the
partnership in those terms.
