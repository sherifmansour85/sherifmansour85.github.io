HAYAKKOM PARTNER KIT — what each file is for
=============================================

Thank you for partnering with Hayakkom. This kit is shared after our agreement is signed (medical partners)
or accepted by email (tourism and hospitality partners). The files are for your website team, so you can show
the partnership on your website and social media. Please use them as they are, without changing colours or wording.

1. hayakkom-logo.png
   The official Hayakkom logo, for a "Partners" or "International clients" page or a post announcing the
   partnership. Keep some empty space around it; do not stretch or recolour it.

2. hayakkom-partner-badge.png / hayakkom-partner-badge.svg
   A "Partner of Hayakkom" badge for your website footer or international-clients page.
   The PNG works everywhere; the SVG stays sharp at any size.

3. hayakkom-partner-snippet.txt
   Ready-to-paste website code: it shows the badge and links it to www.hayakkom.com.
   Your webmaster pastes it into the page where the badge should appear. No other set-up is needed.

4. About Hayakkom — text you may publish next to the badge (English)
   "Hayakkom coordinates care and travel in Egypt for clients from the GCC countries and worldwide.
   Hayakom for Healthcare Solutions and Logistic Services — Egyptian Commercial Register 49973.
   www.hayakkom.com"

   نبذة عن حياكم — نص يمكن نشره بجانب الشارة (عربي)
   «حياكم تنسّق الرعاية والسفر في مصر للعملاء من دول مجلس التعاون الخليجي ومن جميع أنحاء العالم.
   حياكم لحلول الرعاية الصحية والخدمات اللوجستية — سجل تجاري مصري رقم 49973.
   www.hayakkom.com»

Please describe the partnership in these terms: Hayakkom coordinates and markets; it is not a hospital,
clinic, tourism company or travel agency, and it does not take payments from clients. Your own services,
prices and licence details stay as they are on your website.
