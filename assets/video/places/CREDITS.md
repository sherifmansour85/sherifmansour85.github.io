# Location clips

Short, silent clips shown on destination pages by the HK-CLIP block in `assets/hk-share.js`.
All footage is stock video from Pexels, used under the Pexels licence (free for commercial use, no attribution required).
Each file is trimmed, cropped to 16:9, scaled to 1280x720 and compressed.

| File | Shows | Pexels video IDs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sharm.mp4 | Sharm el-Sheikh coast and resorts from the air | 4185242, 4185239 |
| luxor.mp4 | Temple of Hatshepsut, Luxor West Bank | 30843329, 11276525 |
| nile.mp4 | Nile cruise boat and a felucca sailing boat | 35695256, 35879466 |

Source page for an ID: https://www.pexels.com/video/<ID>/
To add a location: add the .mp4 and .webp poster here, then add one line to the CLIPS map in HK-CLIP.
