Dental Tourism in Egypt — Save 60% on World-Class Treatment

Dental treatment has become one of the most common reasons Gulf patients travel to Cairo. With internationally trained dentists, modern clinics and costs far below Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait, dental tourism in Egypt offers exceptional value without compromising on quality.

Which dental treatments are available in Egypt?

Dental implants · porcelain veneers · full-mouth rehabilitation · crowns · whitening · cosmetic dentistry · clear aligners and orthodontics · root canal treatment · bridges · Hollywood smile.

Egypt’s best dental clinics use the same materials and techniques as clinics in Europe and the Gulf — at a fraction of the cost.

How does the process work with Hayakkom?

1. Contact Hayakkom with your dental needs
2. We arrange a consultation with a suitable clinic
3. The dentist reviews your case and provides a treatment plan
4. We coordinate your travel and accommodation
5. Treatment is carried out during your stay
6. Follow-up coordination is arranged where needed
