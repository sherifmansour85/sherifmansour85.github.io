The Best Day Trips from Cairo — Pyramids, Saqqara, and Beyond

Cairo is the perfect base for exploring some of the world's most extraordinary ancient sites. Whether you have one day or a week, there's an incredible day trip waiting — from the iconic Pyramids of Giza to the lesser-visited but equally remarkable Saqqara complex.

The Pyramids of Giza and Sphinx

The obvious first choice — and rightly so. The Pyramids are best experienced early morning (before 9 AM) or late afternoon when crowds thin and the light is golden. A private guide makes a significant difference, explaining the history, pointing out details tourists miss, and keeping vendors at bay.

Half day: Pyramids + Sphinx + Solar Boat Museum Full day: Add Saqqara and Memphis for an extraordinary value

Saqqara — Egypt's oldest pyramid complex

Just 30km south of Cairo, Saqqara is significantly less crowded than Giza but equally impressive. Home to the Step Pyramid of Djoser — the world's oldest pyramid — and dozens of mastaba tombs with beautifully preserved hieroglyphics.

Many visitors who combine Giza and Saqqara say Saqqara was the highlight. Allow 3-4 hours.

Islamic Cairo and Khan el-Khalili

Medieval Islamic Cairo is one of the world's great urban heritage areas. The Al-Azhar Mosque, Ibn Tulun Mosque, Sultan Hassan complex, and the winding lanes of the Khan el-Khalili bazaar are unmissable.

Best visited in the morning or late afternoon. The bazaar comes alive in the evening. Ideal for spices, perfumes, lanterns, silver, and traditional Egyptian crafts.

Coptic Cairo

A completely different face of Egypt — ancient Christian Cairo with the Hanging Church, Ben Ezra Synagogue, and the Church of Saints Sergius and Bacchus. Usually combined with Islamic Cairo for a full-day tour.

Alexandria — day trip by train

Cairo to Alexandria by air-conditioned train is 2.5 hours. The Mediterranean city offers the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Qaitbay Citadel, Roman catacombs, fresh seafood, and a completely different atmosphere from Cairo. Best as a full-day trip.

Tips for day trips from Cairo

• Always use a licensed guide — Hayakkom arranges this for you • Book your guide the evening before for morning trips • Bring water, sunscreen, and comfortable shoes • Start early — sites get crowded and hot by midday • Avoid independent taxi drivers at major sites — use pre-arranged transport • Photography inside most sites requires a separate ticket
