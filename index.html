Medical Coordination · Conferences · Logistics

Hayakom for Healthcare Solutions and Logistic Services — an Egyptian single-person limited liability company registered under Egyptian Law No. 159 of 1981. We provide administrative and marketing consultancy for medical services, appointment and booking coordination with hospitals and medical centres, organisation of medical conferences and events, and logistics services for Gulf travellers.

Hayakkom is not a hospital, clinic or healthcare provider, and does not practise insurance or brokerage activity. All medical and dental treatment is delivered by independent licensed Egyptian healthcare providers. Travel, tour and transport arrangements are fulfilled by licensed third-party Egyptian operators. Hayakkom does not guarantee medical outcomes.
