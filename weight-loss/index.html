Procedures coordinated

Laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (تكميم المعدة) · Gastric bypass and mini bypass / OAGB · Gastric balloon — non-surgical and reversible · SASI / loop bipartition · Revision surgery after a previous bariatric procedure

Eligibility is a clinical decision, not a booking decision

Bariatric surgery is assessed against your BMI, associated conditions such as type 2 diabetes or hypertension, previous weight-loss attempts, and your general fitness for anaesthesia. Not everyone who wants surgery is a candidate, and the choice between sleeve and bypass is made by the surgeon on clinical grounds — never on price. Hayakkom does not assess eligibility; the treating surgeon does.

Risks you should know before you travel

These are real operations with real risks. Published complications include bleeding, staple-line leak, infection, long-term nutritional deficiencies requiring lifelong supplementation, and reflux. Roughly 10–20% of patients experience significant weight regain over time. Excess or loose skin after major weight loss is common and may need separate procedures. Long-term success depends heavily on sustained dietary and lifestyle change, not on the surgery alone.

What Hayakkom coordinates

Surgeon and hospital selection · Pre-operative investigations and anaesthetic clearance scheduling · Hospital admission (typically 1–2 nights) · Recovery-appropriate accommodation with suitable food options · Airport and hospital transfers · Arabic-speaking accompaniment · Dietitian follow-up coordination · Structured post-return follow-up with the surgical team

Realistic stay and recovery

Plan 7–10 days in Egypt: pre-operative tests, surgery, 1–2 hospital nights, then monitored recovery before you are cleared to fly. You will be on a staged diet — liquids, then puréed, then soft food over several weeks. You should not book a demanding travel itinerary around this procedure.

What we will not do

We will not arrange bariatric surgery for anyone the surgeon has not cleared, we will not quote a final price before clinical assessment, and we do not guarantee any amount of weight loss. Anyone promising a fixed kilogram outcome is not being straight with you.
