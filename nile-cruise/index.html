Nile River Cruises

Sail through ancient history on a luxury Nile cruise — from Cairo feluccas to multi-day Luxor-Aswan voyages.

Cruise options

Felucca sunset sailing (Cairo) · Day cruise (Luxor or Aswan) · Luxor to Aswan 3-4 night cruise · Aswan to Luxor 3-4 night cruise · Luxury 5-7 night cruise with all sites

What to expect

Karnak & Luxor Temples · Valley of the Kings · Edfu & Horus Temple · Kom Ombo · Philae Temple · Nubian villages · All meals, guided excursions, and on-board entertainment included

Best time to cruise

October to April: ideal temperatures (20-28°C). May to September: very hot but cruises still run. Christmas and Easter are busiest — book in advance.
