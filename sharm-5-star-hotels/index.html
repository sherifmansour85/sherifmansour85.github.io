5-Star Hotels in Sharm El Sheikh for Gulf Families: Areas, Prices and How We Book

How to choose the right 5-star resort in Sharm el-Sheikh — area, beach, house reef, children’s facilities and price — with a written, itemised quote in riyals.

In short: a 5-night family stay in a 5-star resort in Sharm el-Sheikh typically costs 3,500–9,700 SAR per room (2 adults), excluding flights, depending on the hotel, the dates, the room type and the board. We shortlist 5-star resorts that match your children’s ages and quote everything in Saudi riyals.

Choose your area first

Naama BayNabq BaySharks BayRas NasraniHadabaRas Um Sid

Naama Bay — the town’s centre, with a walkable promenade, restaurants and shops; hotels here usually have a sandy bay. Nabq Bay — north, close to the airport, with large newer resorts, water parks inside some hotels and wide grounds. Sharks Bay and Ras Nasrani — quieter cliffside hotels with some of the best house reefs for snorkelling straight from the hotel. Hadaba and Ras Um Sid — south, near the Old Market, with reef access and many options. For toddlers we look for a sandy beach or a protected lagoon; for snorkelling teenagers, a strong house reef.

How prices work

Hotels set their own rates and publish them on their official websites and booking pages. These change daily with the dates, the room and the availability, so a number copied today can be wrong tomorrow. For every hotel you ask about, we check the current rate for your exact dates and send it to you in writing, in SAR, with the date it applies to and what it includes.

Questions families ask about 5-star hotels in Sharm el-Sheikh

Which area of Sharm el-Sheikh is best for families?

Naama Bay suits families who like to walk to restaurants and shops in the evening. Nabq Bay, close to the airport, has large newer resorts with wide grounds and, in some hotels, water parks. Sharks Bay and Ras Nasrani have quieter cliffside hotels with strong house reefs. We shortlist by your children’s ages and what the family enjoys.

How much does a 5-star hotel in Sharm el-Sheikh cost?

A 5-night family stay in a 5-star resort typically ranges from 3,500 to 9,700 SAR per room (2 adults), excluding flights. The price depends on the hotel, the dates, the room type and the board type. We send a written, itemised quote in SAR.

Are all 5-star hotels in Sharm all-inclusive?

No. Board type varies by hotel and by rate — all-inclusive, half board or bed and breakfast. We check what your rate includes, in writing, before you pay.

Which hotel has the best house reef for snorkelling?

Sharks Bay and Ras Nasrani are known for some of the best house reefs, so children and teenagers can snorkel straight from the hotel. In Nabq Bay, shallow reef flats mean many hotels use jetties to reach deeper water.

How do you choose the hotels you suggest?

How far is Sharm el-Sheikh from Saudi Arabia?

About two hours by air from Riyadh or Jeddah, with direct flights from Saudi Arabia and Kuwait. Flights are not included in our indicative prices.

What do 5-star hotel prices in Sharm el-Sheikh depend on?
