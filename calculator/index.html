Medical Trip Cost Calculator

Most people planning treatment in Egypt know the price of the procedure and not the cost of the trip. This adds what usually gets left out: flights from your city, accommodation, transfers, and the number of trips — dental implants need two, which doubles flights and hotels.

These are indicative ranges for planning only. Hayakkom does not set treatment prices — the treating provider does, and confirms the cost after clinical assessment. Flight and hotel rates vary by season and booking date.

Why dental implants need two trips

The implant is placed on the first trip. The bone then needs time to heal around it — commonly two to six months depending on the case — and the crown is fitted on the second trip. Anyone budgeting for a single trip is planning for roughly half the real cost.

Immediate-loading options allow a temporary bridge on the same day, but the final restoration is fitted after healing. Which approach suits you is a decision for the treating dentist.

Questions

Dental treatment in Egypt — common questions

How much do dental implants cost in Egypt?

Published market prices in Egypt run from roughly EGP 7,000 to 42,000 per implant. That is third-party market data, not a quotation from us. The widest variable is the implant system used, then the crown type, then whether bone grafting is needed. Your own cost is confirmed by the treating provider after reviewing your imaging.

Is the crown included in the implant price?

Usually not. The crown is typically priced separately in Egyptian clinics, and this is what catches most patients out. Always ask whether a quoted figure covers implant and crown together, or the implant alone.

How many trips do I need for dental implants?

Implants normally require two trips, months apart. The first places the implant (3-5 days), the bone then needs time to heal around it, and a second trip fits the crown (3-5 days). Immediate loading is not suitable for every case and is a decision your dentist makes after examination and imaging.

What imaging do I need before travelling?

A panoramic X-ray usually, and for implant cases a CBCT scan. Recent images from your dentist at home are often enough for an initial review, and save you time and cost after arrival.

Can I get a price before I travel?

A preliminary estimate, yes. You send imaging and reports, a licensed provider reviews them, and you receive an indicative plan, cost estimate and expected length of stay before booking a flight. The final figure is confirmed after direct examination.

What is the difference between Korean, German and Swiss implants?

The implant system's country of origin is the biggest driver of price. Which suits you is a clinical decision your dentist makes based on bone condition and treatment plan. But you are entitled to know which system will be used and what it costs, in writing, before you travel.

When is bone grafting or a sinus lift needed?

When bone density or height is insufficient to hold the implant securely. This is determined from CT imaging and adds to both cost and duration - which is why a final price cannot be given from a photograph.

Can I fly straight after the procedure?

For most dental work yes, but the decision belongs to the treating dentist. We schedule your return after the provider confirms you are fit to travel.

Who follows up after I return home?

We coordinate follow-up between you and the treating provider. Make sure you leave with a written report including the implant system used - your dentist at home will need it for any future work on that tooth.

Why do prices differ so much between Egyptian clinics?

The implant system, crown material (zirconia, E-max or porcelain), the dentist's experience, the facility standard, and whether preparatory work such as bone grafting is required.
