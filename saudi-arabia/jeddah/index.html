Medical Treatment in Egypt from Jeddah

Jeddah has the shortest flight and the most departures, which makes it the easiest city for treatment needing two separate trips — dental implants in particular, where placement and crown fitting are months apart. Hair restoration, dentistry, weight-loss surgery and cosmetic surgery with licensed Egyptian providers — coordinated in Arabic, from your first message to your follow-up after you return to the Kingdom.

Why Egypt

Why Saudi patients travel to Egypt for treatment

01

A short flight, not a long-haul journey

Cairo is 2 to 3 hours from the major Saudi cities, with daily direct flights. That matters more than it sounds: it is a short trip for a companion, a realistic second trip for treatment needing two stages, and a manageable return if you need further review.

02

No language barrier at any point

You are dealt with in Arabic throughout — the first enquiry, your records, the treatment explanation, the hospital paperwork and the follow-up. For a medical decision, understanding exactly what is being proposed matters more than almost anything else.

03

Meaningful cost difference on private treatment

Private treatment in Egypt is substantially less expensive than equivalent private care in the Kingdom, particularly for elective dentistry, hair restoration and cosmetic surgery. We publish sourced market ranges rather than promises, so you can judge for yourself before travelling.

04

Established capacity for international patients

The hospitals and clinics we coordinate with routinely treat Gulf patients and understand what that involves — scheduling around travel, family accompanying the patient, and documentation you can take home to your own doctor.

05

Somewhere your family can actually be

Companions travelling with you are not confined to a hospital waiting room. Where your recovery allows it, and only with your physician's approval, there is room for your family to see something of Egypt while you rest.

Practical notes

Things worth knowing before you come

Time difference

Saudi Arabia is normally one hour ahead of Egypt. During Egyptian summer time the two are the same. Worth checking when you agree appointment times with us.

Currency

The Egyptian Pound floats, so rates move. The Saudi Riyal is pegged to the US Dollar. For that reason we quote indicative figures in SAR or USD rather than in Pounds, which would be out of date quickly.

Where you will be treated

Mostly Cairo and Giza, where our partner hospitals and clinics are. Recovery and wellness stays can be arranged on the Red Sea or elsewhere once your physician clears you to travel domestically.

Phone and contact

We hold a Saudi number so you are not making an international call to ask a question, and WhatsApp is the channel most patients actually use.

Saudi patient questions

Questions we are asked most from the Kingdom

Can I be assessed before I travel?

Yes, and you should be. Send your records and an appropriate licensed provider reviews them before you book flights. You then know the likely treatment pathway, how long to stay and an estimated cost — and critically, whether you are a candidate at all. Travelling first and asking afterwards is how people waste money.

How long will I need to be away from home?

It depends entirely on the procedure. Hair transplant 4 to 6 days. Dental veneers 5 to 7 days. Dental implants usually two trips of 3 to 5 days, months apart. Bariatric surgery 7 to 10 days. Cosmetic surgery 7 to 14 days, because your surgeon must clear you as fit to fly before you travel.

Will everything be in Arabic?

Yes. Coordination, your documents, accompaniment at appointments and follow-up are all handled in Arabic. English is available if you prefer it.

Can my wife and children come with me?

Yes, and most patients bring family. We arrange accommodation suitable for a family rather than a single traveller, transport, and activities for companions that fit around your treatment schedule.

Can I see prices in Saudi Riyal?

Yes. Every price range on the site can be switched to SAR, and the Riyal is pegged to the US Dollar, so those conversions are stable rather than moving day to day.

Who actually performs my treatment?

An independent licensed Egyptian physician or surgeon at a licensed facility. Hayakkom coordinates access, appointments and logistics. We are not a hospital or clinic, we do not diagnose or treat, and we do not guarantee medical outcomes.

What happens if the treatment needs more time than planned?

Your coordinator handles it — rescheduling appointments, extending accommodation, and helping change your flight. This is one of the more common reasons people prefer coordination to arranging everything themselves from another country.

What happens after I get back to Saudi Arabia?

You leave Egypt with a written treatment summary, your medications and an agreed follow-up schedule. If something comes up at home, we coordinate with the treating provider, and help arrange further review or a second trip when the treatment requires one.

What should I bring with me?

Your medical records and any imaging, a list of current medications with doses, your insurance details if relevant, and comfortable loose clothing if you are having surgery. We send a specific list once your pathway is confirmed, because it differs by procedure.

What about entry requirements?

Entry rules change, so we will not state them on a web page as though they are fixed. Confirm current requirements with the Egyptian authorities or your airline before booking, and we will advise on the documentation your treatment itself requires.
